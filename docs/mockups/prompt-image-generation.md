# Prompts image · générateurs IA

Prompts textuels prêts à coller dans un générateur d'images IA (Claude design, Nano Banana, Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion) pour obtenir des variantes peintes plus riches du mockup SVG. Utile pour explorer le rendu final visé sans passer par une modélisation 3D complète.

## Consignes générales pour tous les prompts

Coller ces contraintes globales en tête si le générateur les respecte :

```
Isometric orthographic camera angle, 30 degree tilt, 45 degree azimuth.
Cel-shaded low-poly art style. Ligne claire. Minimalist theatrical
composition inspired by Sable (Shedworks), Journey (thatgamecompany),
Kentucky Route Zero (Cardboard Computer). No text. No UI. No HUD.
Silhouettes clean and readable. No PBR, no metallic surfaces.
Palette strictly limited to: ochre warm #C89060, ochre deep #A65F35,
granite light #B5B0A8, granite dark #4A4844, slate sea #3D4A55,
night blue #1B2432, dusk gold #E8A952, fire orange #F0783C,
earth red #8C3E28, bone white #E8DFC8.
```

## Prompt 1 · Acte 1 · Campement au crépuscule

**Cible : mockup principal, référence visuelle du projet**

```
Isometric video game concept art, Neolithic Brittany campsite at dusk,
around 4500 BC, in the coastal moor of Morbihan.

Central scene: a burning campfire with warm orange glow rendered as soft
radial light on ground. Around the fire:
- A young woman sitting cross-legged in front of the fire, dark brown
  skin, long dark hair loose, warm ochre paint marks on cheekbones,
  tanned leather clothing.
- An elderly man standing behind the fire, curved posture leaning on a
  wooden staff, very dark skin, white hair and beard, deep ochre robe,
  abstract tattoos on arms.
- A tall young man crouching to the right, gathering shells from the
  ground, dark brown skin, hair tied back with fibers, deep ochre
  clothing, a woven net at his belt.
- A small child running to the left, ochre skin, dark braided hair,
  light blue eyes, laughing, chasing a domesticated grey goat.

Background right: two conical thatched huts with dark entrance openings,
built of wood and daub, positioned mid-distance.

Background: horizontal band of slate-grey sea with subtle golden
reflections. Sky: gradient from ochre warm at horizon to deep night blue
at top, single prominent star rising in the east on the right side of
the sky, subtle warm glow around it.

Foreground: dry ochre earth ground, sparse tufts of tall grass, scattered
granite rocks, some yellow gorse flowers.

Lighting: warm dusk directional light from the west casting long soft
shadows to the east. Firelight adds warm orange point light on nearby
figures. Palette strictly limited as above. Style: Sable game meets
Journey game.
```

## Prompt 2 · Acte 2 · Rencontre inter-tribu

**Cible : moment charnière narratif**

```
Isometric orthographic video game concept art, Neolithic Brittany, a
narrow moor path near coastal cliffs, late afternoon light, warm slanting
sun casting long shadows.

Two women from one tribe (dark skin, ochre facial markings, tanned
leather clothing) walking on the path from the left, one carrying a
woven basket, both slightly tense. On the path ahead of them, two
strangers from another tribe (very dark skin, red and white body paint,
different leather cut, longer wooden staff), one standing still and
staring at an isolated boulder beside the path, hand placed on the stone,
eyes closed.

Composition centered on the boulder itself, both groups meeting around
it without touching. Cliff edge and slate sea visible in background
right. Yellow gorse flowers in foreground.

Atmosphere: suspended time, silence, ritualistic quality.
Palette dominant: dusk gold, ochre warm, granite mid, slate sea.
Style: Sable game, Journey game, minimalist theatrical composition.
No text, no UI, no HUD, no combat, no weapons.
```

## Prompt 3 · Acte 3 · La première pierre

**Cible : climax du MVP, moment fondateur**

```
Isometric orthographic video game concept art, Neolithic Brittany, dawn.
A grassy clearing at the edge of a slate-grey sea. Sky pale, gradient
from cold blue-violet to warm ochre at horizon. Single prominent star
rising in the east.

Central figure: a young woman with dark brown skin, long dark hair, warm
ochre paint on cheekbones, tanned leather clothing, standing alone facing
the sea and the star. She has just placed a long flat stone upright in
the ground before her. The stone stands vertical, faces east, casts a
subtle blue-violet halo shader glow around itself.

Behind her, at a respectful distance, her tribe stands silent: an elderly
man leaning on a staff (absent in reality, only visible as a memory
outline in soft warm light), a tall young man kneeling and placing a
second smaller stone next to hers, a small child touching the standing
stone with fingertips.

Foreground: dew-covered grass, granite outcrops, mist.
Palette dominant: bone white, granite light, cool halo blue, warm ochre
at horizon.

Atmosphere: reverence, birth of a rite, first gesture ever done. No text,
no UI, no HUD, no dialogue balloons. Style: Sable meets Journey meets a
sacred moment. Ligne claire, cel-shaded low-poly.
```

## Prompt 4 · Peinture pariétale (fresque de fin Acte 3)

**Cible : signature graphique unique du jeu**

```
Neolithic European cave painting style, in the manner of Lascaux, Chauvet,
and Levantine rock art. Trembling hand-drawn line, 2 to 4 pixels thick.
Palette strictly: charcoal black, warm ochre, earth red, bone white only.
Rough calcareous stone surface texture with imperfections.

Subject: single silhouette of a standing human figure with hair, facing
right, arms at sides. In front of the figure, a tall vertical stone stands
upright. Above the stone, an eight-pointed star. To the right, an
undulating line representing the sea.

Composition centered, empty space around. No modern iconography. No
photorealism. Style must look indistinguishable from an actual archaeological
tracing at first glance. Style reference: relevés de Lascaux, art rupestre
du Levant espagnol néolithique.
```

**Cible : dispositif méta-temporel du jeu**

```
Horizontal timeline banner, video game title screen, wide aspect ratio 21:9.

Left third: deep prehistoric symbols, hand-drawn charcoal and ochre style,
suggesting Paleolithic hunter-gatherers. Middle: symbols become more

Palette strictly limited to charter section 3. No modern typography visible
in this prompt (typography added in-engine).
Style: minimalist theatrical, hand-drawn hybrid vector, Kentucky Route Zero

```

## Comment utiliser ces prompts

1. Choisir un prompt selon la scène visée
2. Coller intégralement dans le générateur (Nano Banana, Midjourney, DALL-E, Claude design, SDXL, etc.)
3. Générer 4 à 8 variantes, sélectionner les 1-2 plus alignées avec la charte
4. Comparer avec le mockup SVG correspondant (composition doit correspondre)
5. Si écart : ajuster le prompt (renforcer contraintes de palette, ajouter "no X" pour éliminer artefacts)
6. Sauvegarder la ou les images retenues dans `docs/mockups/generated/` (dossier à créer)
7. Ces images générées ne remplacent PAS le mockup SVG (référence de composition), mais servent de **cible de rendu final** pour la production 3D

## Notes sur les générateurs

- **Claude design** : bon pour respect contraintes textuelles et cohérence palette. Peut hallucinier sur composition iso.
- **Midjourney** : superbe rendu artistique, mais souvent dérive vers photoréalisme ou styles hors charte. Utiliser `--style raw --stylize 100` pour rester proche du prompt.
- **DALL-E** : bon pour concepts clairs, peut être trop lisse. Répéter les mots-clés "cel-shaded, low-poly, ligne claire" en début et fin de prompt.
- **Stable Diffusion (SDXL / Flux)** : plus contrôlable si tu maîtrises les LoRAs. Chercher un LoRA "isometric game art" ou "cel shading".
- **Nano Banana** : moins connu, tester en priorité si disponible chez toi.

## Consigne finale

Aucune image générée ne doit être diffusée publiquement sans mention explicite qu'elle est produite par IA. La règle vaut aussi pour toute image que tu ne peux pas librement redistribuer (droits d'auteur ambigus pour beaucoup de générateurs). Pour un mockup interne au repo, aucun problème.
